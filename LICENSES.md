## Master and runtime files

The 11 GLBs in `assets-src/cars/` are the preserved master assets. The build pipeline creates separate runtime derivatives in `public/assets/cars/`: LOD0–LOD3, convex collision GLBs, and wheel-rig JSON. Runtime vehicle LODs are normalized to metres/Y-up/+Z-forward/ground-zero, Meshopt-compressed, WebP-textured, and progressively simplified. These generated files and the rendered WebP previews remain derivatives of the original CC BY 4.0 vehicles and carry the same attribution obligations.

Collision and rig outputs are technical starting assets, not a completed vehicle physics or gameplay system.
